### Entities candidates:

#### People:

- **Johnathan "Johnny" Hammersmith** - A controversial tech billionaire known for his eccentric behavior and flamboyant public appearances.

- **Amelia Spector** - A prominent politician who recently announced her campaign for the presidency.

- **Professor Lila Drake** - An acclaimed scientist leading the research on a groundbreaking AI technology.

- **Detective Sam Doyle** - A famous investigator who's recently cracked a high-profile case involving cybercrime.

#### Places:

- **Brightwood** - A bustling city known for its technological innovations and vibrant nightlife.

- **Thornfield University** - A prestigious institution of higher education embroiled in a recent academic scandal.

- **Serenity Bay** - A popular tourist destination with unspoiled beaches and natural beauty.

- **Castleton Towers** - A high-end residential complex rumored to house some of the world's wealthiest individuals.

#### Organizations:

- **Orion Technologies** - A leading tech company in Brightwood, currently under investigation for potential data privacy violations.

- **The Emerald Coalition** - An influential political organization supporting environmental causes.

- **United Citizens Foundation** - A non-profit organization focusing on promoting social justice and equality.

- **The Nova Group** - A mysterious organization suspected of manipulating global financial markets.

  

#### Events:

- **The Brightwood Annual Tech Expo** - A renowned tech event where Orion Technologies is set to reveal a breakthrough product.

- **The Spector Presidential Campaign** - A high-stakes political event marked by promises of drastic policy changes.

- **The Serenity Bay Summer Festival** - A major tourist event attracting people from around the globe.

- **The Thornfield University Scandal** - A controversial event involving allegations of academic misconduct by top officials.

  
  

### Themes candidates:

1. **Technology Innovation** - Pertaining to the advancements made in Brightwood, and the breakthroughs from Orion Technologies or Johnathan Hammersmith's ventures.

2. **Data Privacy** - Relating to the investigation into Orion Technologies for potential privacy violations.

3. **Politics** - Related to Amelia Spector's presidential campaign, the Emerald Coalition's activities, or potential political influence on the Orion Technologies investigation.

4. **Environmental Policy** - With respect to the efforts of the Emerald Coalition, or policy proposals from Amelia Spector's campaign.

5. **Wealth and Class** - Connected to the lifestyles of Johnathan "Johnny" Hammersmith, the residents of Brightwood, and the potential implications of Amelia Spector's policy proposals on wealth distribution.

6. **Education** - Related to Thornfield University, its prestige, and the academic scandal involving the institution.

7. **Scandal and Controversy** - Surrounding the academic scandal at Thornfield University, the data privacy investigation into Orion Technologies, and potential controversies involving Johnny Hammersmith or Amelia Spector's campaign.

8. **Urban Development** - Linked to the urbanization, infrastructure, and lifestyle of Brightwood.

9. **Election Campaigns** - As it pertains to Amelia Spector's presidential campaign and the potential influence of various entities.

10. **Corporate Ethics** - In connection with Orion Technologies and its data privacy issues.
